Where AI is used — and where it is deliberately excluded

AI adds value

  • Contract extraction — unstructured prose into structure; regex fails on the second contract
  • Explanation — turning a nested decision trace into language a controller can read
  • Entity resolution — "Orient Electric" vs "OEL" vs "ORIENT ELEC LTD - BLR"
  • Anomaly narration — hypothesising why two systems diverge

AI excluded by design

  • Computing any amount — must be reproducible and defensible to an auditor
  • Deciding to block billing — must be versioned and replayable
  • Resolving authority conflicts — a written policy, not a runtime judgement
  • Confirming an extraction — confidence never substitutes for human confirmation on money
The principle: AI proposes, deterministic code disposes, and anything touching money is deterministic.
