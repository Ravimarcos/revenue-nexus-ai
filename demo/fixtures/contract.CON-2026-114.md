## 1. Definitions

1.1 **"Active Employee"** means an individual enrolled by the Customer on the Benefits Wallet product in a Billing Period.

1.2 **"Billing Period"** means a calendar month.

1.3 **"Acceptance Window"** means the period following delivery of a milestone during which the Customer may raise objections, as specified in clause 4.2.

---

## 2. Payment terms

2.1 All invoices are payable within **forty-five (45) days** of the invoice date.

2.2 All amounts are exclusive of Goods and Services Tax, which shall be charged at the prevailing rate. Place of supply is Karnataka.

2.3 Tax deducted at source by the Customer shall be evidenced by a certificate issued within the statutory timeframe.

---

## 3. Platform charges

### 3.1 Benefits Wallet — subscription

The Customer subscribes to the Benefits Wallet product for **five hundred (500) Active Employees** at **₹1,200 per Active Employee per month**, invoiced **monthly in advance**.

> *Amended by A1 §2 with effect from 16 July 2026 — see Amendment A1.*

### 3.4 Channel Rewards — usage charges

The Customer is entitled to **2,000,000 reward transactions per Billing Period** at no additional charge.

Transactions in excess of the included volume shall be charged on a tiered basis, **the applicable tier being determined by the total volume consumed in the Billing Period**:

| Total volume in Billing Period | Rate per transaction |
|---|---|
| Up to 1,000,000 | ₹0.08 |
| 1,000,001 – 5,000,000 | ₹0.06 |
| Above 5,000,000 | ₹0.04 |

Usage charges are invoiced **monthly in arrears**.

> *Amended by A1 §3 with effect from 16 July 2026 — see Amendment A1.*

> **Note on clause 3.4.** The phrase *"the applicable tier being determined by the total volume consumed"* is the explicit tier interpretation. Ordinary contract language frequently omits this. Without it, the same overage prices differently depending on whether bands are measured against total or excess volume — a material divergence on identical text.

## 4. Implementation services

### 4.1 Scope

The Supplier shall deliver Spend Automation implementation services for a fixed fee of **₹40,00,000 (Rupees forty lakh only)**.

### 4.2 Milestone billing and acceptance

The implementation fee shall be invoiced against the following milestones:

| Milestone | Description | Share | Value |
|---|---|---|---|
| M1 | Project kickoff and mobilisation | 20% | ₹8,00,000 |
| M2 | Solution design and architecture sign-off | 20% | ₹8,00,000 |
| M3 | User acceptance testing sign-off | 30% | ₹12,00,000 |
| M4 | Production go-live | 30% | ₹12,00,000 |

**A milestone shall become invoiceable only upon expiry of an Acceptance Window of thirty (30) days from the date of Customer sign-off**, during which the Customer may raise written objections. Where objections are raised, the milestone shall not become invoiceable until resolved.

> **This clause is why ₹12,00,000 is held in the July 2026 run.** M3 was signed off on 14 July 2026. The Acceptance Window expires on 13 August 2026.

## 5. One-time charges

5.1 A one-time charge of **₹2,50,000 (Rupees two lakh fifty thousand only)** is payable for platform onboarding and data migration, invoiced on activation.

## 6. Term and renewal

6.1 This Agreement runs for an initial term of twenty-four (24) months from 1 April 2026.

6.2 **This Agreement shall renew automatically for successive periods of twenty-four (24) months** unless either party gives written notice of non-renewal not less than **sixty (60) days** before the end of the then-current term.

> The notice window for the first renewal opens on 31 January 2028. An auto-renewing contract that passes its notice window without review renews on pricing agreed two years earlier.

---

## 7. Amendment

7.1 This Agreement may be amended only in writing, signed by authorised representatives of both parties. **Amendments take effect from the date specified therein and do not operate retrospectively.**

# AMENDMENT A1

**To:** CON-2026-114 · **Executed:** 14 July 2026 · **Effective:** 16 July 2026

## §1 Recitals

The Customer wishes to expand its enrolled employee base and increase its reward transaction entitlement. The parties agree as follows.

## §2 Amendment to clause 3.1

The number of Active Employees is increased from **500 to 750**.

In consideration of the increased commitment, a **volume discount of 8%** shall apply to the Benefits Wallet subscription charge.

The amended charge is ₹1,200 per Active Employee per month, less 8%.

## §3 Amendment to clause 3.4

The included reward transaction entitlement is increased from **2,000,000 to 3,000,000 per Billing Period**.

The tier structure and the tier determination basis in clause 3.4 are otherwise unchanged.

## §4 Effect

This Amendment takes effect on **16 July 2026** and does not operate retrospectively. Charges for the period 1 July 2026 to 15 July 2026 remain governed by the unamended terms.

> **§4 is the operative clause for the July 2026 billing run.** Because the amendment does not operate retrospectively, the July Billing Period must be decomposed into two rating segments — 1–15 July under base terms, 16–31 July under A1 — and each rated independently. Rating the period as a single unit under either set of terms produces a materially incorrect result.
